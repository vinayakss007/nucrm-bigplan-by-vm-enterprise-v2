## Overview

Deal activities capture every interaction that moves an opportunity forward. Logging calls, meetings, emails, and notes on deals creates an auditable history that any team member can reference. This helps with handoffs, deal reviews, and understanding what led to a win or loss.

## Types of activities

NuCRM tracks several activity types on deals:

| Activity type | What it records |
|--------------|-----------------|
| Call | Phone conversation details, outcome, duration |
| Meeting | In-person or virtual meeting summary |
| Email | Messages sent/received (auto-logged from sync) |
| Note | Internal team observations and context |
| Task | Actions that need to be completed |
| SMS/WhatsApp | Messages sent via these channels |

## Logging a call

When you finish a phone call about a deal:

1. Open the deal detail page
2. Click **Log call** in the action bar (or press the phone icon)
3. Fill in the call details:
   - **Subject** - Brief description (for example, "Discovery call with Sarah")
   - **Direction** - Inbound or outbound
   - **Duration** - How long the call lasted
   - **Outcome** - Connected, voicemail, no answer, busy
   - **Summary** - Key points discussed and next steps
4. Click **Save**

The call appears on the deal timeline with all logged details.

<Callout variant="tip">
Log calls immediately after they happen while details are fresh. Even a brief two-sentence summary is better than no record at all. Future you (or a colleague taking over the deal) will appreciate the context.
</Callout>

### Call outcomes

Track the result of every call attempt:

- **Connected** - Spoke with the intended person
- **Left voicemail** - Left a message
- **No answer** - Call was not picked up and no voicemail left
- **Busy/callback requested** - Person asked to be called back later
- **Wrong number** - Contact information needs updating

## Logging a meeting

Record details from a meeting (virtual or in-person):

1. Open the deal page
2. Click **Log meeting** (or find it under the "+" menu)
3. Enter:
   - **Subject** - Meeting topic
   - **Date and time** - When it occurred
   - **Duration** - How long it lasted
   - **Attendees** - Who was present (from your team and the prospect's side)
   - **Location** - Office address, video call link, or phone
   - **Notes** - Detailed summary of what was discussed
   - **Outcome** - Positive, neutral, or follow-up needed
4. Click **Save**

<Callout variant="note">
Meetings logged on a deal also appear on the timelines of all linked contacts. You do not need to separately log the same meeting on each contact record.
</Callout>

## Adding notes

Notes capture internal context that does not fit into a structured call or meeting log:

### Creating a note

1. On the deal page, click the **Notes** tab or click **Add note** in the timeline
2. Type your note using the rich text editor
3. Formatting options include:
   - Bold, italic, underline
   - Bullet and numbered lists
   - Headers
   - @mentions of team members
   - Links
4. Click **Save**

### When to use notes

- Recording insights from informal conversations
- Documenting competitor intelligence gathered during calls
- Noting internal strategy decisions about the deal
- Summarizing email threads too complex for the auto-log
- Leaving context for a teammate who will handle next steps

### @Mentions in notes

Type **@** followed by a teammate's name to notify them:

- They receive an in-app notification
- The notification links directly to the deal and note
- Useful for flagging deals that need attention or handing off tasks

<Callout variant="info">
Notes are internal and never visible to the customer or prospect. Use them freely for candid observations, risk assessments, and strategy discussions.
</Callout>

## Email activity

Emails are tracked on deals automatically when email sync is enabled:

### Auto-logged emails

- Emails sent from NuCRM to contacts linked to the deal appear on the timeline
- Inbound replies are also captured
- Thread view groups related messages together

### Manual email association

If an email was sent outside NuCRM but relates to a deal:

1. Forward the email to your NuCRM logging address (found in Settings > Email > Logging address)
2. Include the deal name or ID in the subject line
3. The email is associated with the correct deal automatically

## Activity timeline

All activities appear on the deal timeline in chronological order:

### Timeline features

- **Filter by type** - Show only calls, only emails, only notes, etc.
- **Search** - Find specific content within timeline entries
- **Pin important entries** - Keep critical updates visible at the top
- **Edit** - Modify your own entries after creation
- **Delete** - Remove entries you created (admin can remove any)

### Timeline for deal reviews

During weekly pipeline reviews, scroll through recent timeline entries to:

- Confirm deals have recent activity
- Check that next steps are being followed up
- Identify deals going silent (no entries in 7+ days)
- Review call outcomes and meeting summaries

## Activity reports on deals

Track activity metrics across your pipeline:

| Metric | What it measures |
|--------|-----------------|
| Activities per deal | Average number of touchpoints before close |
| Days since last activity | Identifies stale deals |
| Calls logged this week | Rep productivity indicator |
| Meetings completed | Engagement level with prospects |

Access these reports from **Reports > Sales activity**.

## Best practices

1. **Log every meaningful touchpoint** - Even brief interactions provide context
2. **Use consistent subjects** - Makes timeline scanning faster (for example, "Follow-up call re: pricing")
3. **Note next steps** - Always end a log entry with what happens next and when
4. **Review weekly** - Look for deals with no activity in 7+ days and take action
5. **Leverage @mentions** - Keep teammates informed about deals they support without separate messages
