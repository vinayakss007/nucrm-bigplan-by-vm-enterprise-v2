## What is logged

NuCRM maintains two levels of history:

1. **Audit log** — a workspace-wide, immutable record of every significant action
2. **Change history** — per-record, per-field before/after values

Together they answer: "Who changed the deal value in March, and what was it before?"

## Viewing the audit log

Go to **Settings > Audit** to see a chronological stream of events:

- Record created, updated, deleted, restored
- User invited, role changed, deactivated
- Settings changed (pipeline stages, permissions, modules)
- Login, logout, failed login attempts
- Impersonation sessions started and ended
- Export and bulk operations

Each entry shows:

| Column | Detail |
|---|---|
| Timestamp | When it happened (to the second) |
| User | Who did it (or "System" for automated actions) |
| Action | What was done (create, update, delete, login, export) |
| Entity | Which record or setting was affected |
| Details | Specific fields changed (with old → new values for updates) |

## Change history on a record

Open any contact, deal or ticket and click the **History** tab. You see every field change with:

- The date of the change
- Who made it
- The field name
- The value before
- The value after

This is especially useful for:

- Tracking deal value changes (was it discounted without approval?)
- Seeing who reassigned a contact (and when)
- Proving what was communicated to a customer at a given date

<Callout variant="info">
The audit log is immutable. Not even administrators can delete or edit entries. This is by design — the log's value as evidence depends on nobody being able to tamper with it.
</Callout>

## Filtering and exporting

Filter the audit log by date range, user, action type, or entity. Export filtered results as CSV for compliance evidence or internal review.

## Retention

Audit log entries are retained according to your workspace's retention policy (configurable in Settings). Enterprise plans support extended retention periods.
