## What lead warming does

Lead warming keeps your contacts warm between active sales conversations by sending personalised messages on occasions that matter: festivals (Diwali, Christmas, New Year, Holi and 20+ more), birthdays, and work anniversaries. Messages are generated by the AI assistant in the tone you choose, and delivered across email, WhatsApp or SMS.

## Setting it up

1. Enable the **Lead Warming** module from **Settings > Modules**
2. Go to **Settings > Lead Warming**
3. Choose which occasions to activate (festivals, birthdays, anniversaries)
4. Set the default tone (warm professional, casual friendly, formal, festive)
5. Choose delivery channels (email, WhatsApp, SMS)
6. Define which contacts are eligible (by segment, tag or lifecycle stage)

<Callout variant="note">
Birthday messages require a date-of-birth field on the contact. Anniversary messages use the "created at" date or a custom anniversary field you define.
</Callout>

## How messages are generated

The AI assistant writes each message from the contact's record context — their name, company, your relationship history. You can:

- Let the AI generate fresh each time
- Review and approve before sending (default for the first run)
- Use a template with merge fields instead of AI generation

## Reply-intent detection

When a contact replies to a warming message, the AI analyses the reply and classifies it:

- **Interested** — creates a follow-up task for the owner
- **Not interested** — logs it, applies a cooldown
- **Ask later** — schedules a re-attempt after a delay

This means positive replies become real pipeline actions without anyone monitoring an inbox.

## Rate limiting and cooldown

Contacts are never over-messaged. The system enforces:

- A minimum gap between warming messages to the same contact
- A maximum number of messages per contact per month
- Automatic exclusion of contacts in active deal stages (they are already being talked to)
