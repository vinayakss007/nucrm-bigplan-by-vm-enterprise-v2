## What sequences do

A sequence is an ordered series of emails sent to a contact over time. Each step has a delay (e.g. "wait 3 days"), a template, and personalisation variables. The sequence stops automatically when the contact replies, unsubscribes, or meets an exit condition you define.

## Creating a sequence

1. Go to **Automations > Sequences**
2. Click **New sequence**
3. Name it and set its purpose (e.g. "Post-demo follow-up")
4. Add steps: for each step, choose a delay, pick or create an email template, and set any conditions

### Step configuration

Each step has:

- **Delay** — how long to wait since the previous step (or since enrolment for step 1)
- **Template** — the email to send, with merge fields like `{{first_name}}`, `{{deal_name}}`
- **Send window** — optionally restrict to business hours so emails arrive at sensible times
- **Skip condition** — optionally skip this step if a field meets a criteria (e.g. skip step 3 if deal stage has already advanced)

## Enrolling contacts

Contacts enter a sequence by:

- Manual enrolment from the contact or deal detail view
- A workflow action ("Enrol in sequence")
- A segment rule (everyone matching a filter joins automatically)
- A form submission trigger

<Callout variant="warning">
A contact can only be in one sequence at a time. Enrolling them in a new one exits them from the current one.
</Callout>

## Exit rules

A contact exits automatically when:

- They reply to any email in the sequence
- They reach the end of all steps
- They are manually removed
- An exit condition you defined is met (e.g. deal moves to a later stage)
- They unsubscribe

## Monitoring

The sequence dashboard shows:

- How many contacts are currently enrolled
- Which step each is on
- Open and reply rates per step
- Drop-off and completion rates
