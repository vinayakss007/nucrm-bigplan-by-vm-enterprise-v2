## What the knowledge base provides

A knowledge base (KB) is a self-service library of articles organised by category. Customers search it before submitting a ticket; agents insert articles into replies instead of retyping explanations.

Good KB coverage directly reduces ticket volume.

## Setting up the knowledge base

1. Go to **Knowledge Base** in the sidebar
2. Create **categories** first (e.g. Getting Started, Billing, Account, Troubleshooting)
3. Within each category, create **articles**

### Writing an article

Each article has:

- **Title** — clear and searchable (e.g. "How to reset your password")
- **Category** — where it lives in the navigation
- **Body** — rich text with headings, images, lists and embedded links
- **Status** — draft (not visible to customers) or published

<Callout variant="tip">
Write titles as the question the customer would type. "How do I change my billing address?" beats "Billing address management" for search and recognition.
</Callout>

## Publishing to the portal

Published articles are immediately available on your customer portal. Customers can:

- Browse by category
- Search by keyword
- Click through to the full article

Draft articles are visible only to your team inside the workspace.

## Agent use

When replying to a ticket, agents can:

- Search the KB from within the reply composer
- Insert an article link or its full content inline
- See which articles were already sent to this requester (avoiding repeats)

## Analytics

Track which articles are viewed, which searches return no results (content gap signal), and which articles lead to a ticket being submitted anyway (indicating the article did not solve the problem).
