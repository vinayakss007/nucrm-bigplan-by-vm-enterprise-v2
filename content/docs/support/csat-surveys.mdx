## How CSAT works

When a ticket is resolved, NuCRM can automatically send the requester a satisfaction survey. The customer clicks a link, rates their experience, and optionally leaves a comment. The score attaches to the ticket and rolls up to agent-level and team-level reporting.

## Enabling CSAT

1. Go to **Settings > CSAT**
2. Toggle CSAT surveys on
3. Configure when to send: immediately on resolution, or after a delay (e.g. 1 hour)
4. Customise the survey message (subject line and body text)
5. Choose the rating scale: 1–5 stars, or thumbs up/down

## The survey experience

The customer receives an email with a link to a public page (no login required). They:

1. Rate their experience
2. Optionally leave a written comment
3. Submit

The response is recorded against the ticket and visible to the assigned agent and managers.

<Callout variant="note">
The survey link is unique per ticket and expires after 30 days. One response per ticket — customers cannot re-submit.
</Callout>

## CSAT reporting

View satisfaction data from **Reports > Support > CSAT**:

- Average score by agent, team and time period
- Score distribution (how many 5s vs 1s)
- Comments linked to each score (useful for coaching)
- Trend over time — is satisfaction improving or declining?

## Acting on low scores

Combine CSAT with automation:

- If a score is 1 or 2, create a follow-up task for the manager
- If average CSAT for an agent drops below a threshold, trigger a notification
- Use low-score tickets as coaching material in 1:1s
