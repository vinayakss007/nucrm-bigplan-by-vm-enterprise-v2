## The ticket queue

Support tickets live under **Tickets** in the sidebar. The default view is a filterable table showing subject, requester, status, priority, assigned agent, SLA status and last update.

### Status workflow

Every ticket moves through statuses:

**Open** → **In Progress** → **Waiting on Customer** → **Resolved** → **Closed**

Status changes can happen manually or be driven by automation (e.g. auto-resolve after 48 hours of no reply from the customer).

### Views

Switch between:

- **Table view** — sortable columns, bulk actions, full detail at a glance
- **Kanban view** — columns are statuses; drag tickets to progress them
- **My tickets** — filtered to your assignments only

## Filters and saved views

Filter by:

- Status, priority, assigned agent or team
- SLA status (within target, at risk, breached)
- Requester company or contact
- Tags or custom fields
- Date range (created, last updated, resolved)

Save any filter combination as a named view for quick access from the sidebar.

## Ticket detail

Click a ticket to open its full detail:

- **Public replies** — what the customer sees
- **Internal notes** — visible only to your team (highlighted differently)
- **Timeline** — every status change, assignment, SLA event and linked activity
- **Related records** — the contact, company, deals and invoices connected to this requester

<Callout variant="tip">
Internal notes are for coordination between agents. The customer never sees them, so use them to document investigation steps, escalation reasoning or handoff context.
</Callout>
