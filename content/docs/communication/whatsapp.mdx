## Overview

WhatsApp is the preferred communication channel for millions of businesses worldwide. NuCRM integrates with the WhatsApp Business API so you can send and receive messages, use approved templates, and manage conversations directly from the CRM. Every message is automatically logged on the contact timeline.

## Prerequisites

Before using WhatsApp in NuCRM, you need:

- A WhatsApp Business account (not a personal WhatsApp number)
- A verified business phone number dedicated to WhatsApp
- Approved message templates (required by WhatsApp for initiating conversations)
- The WhatsApp Business API connected to NuCRM (see setup below)

## Setting up the integration

### Connecting WhatsApp Business

1. Go to **Settings > Integrations > WhatsApp**
2. Click **Connect WhatsApp Business**
3. Follow the guided setup:
   - Verify your business with Meta (parent company of WhatsApp)
   - Select or register a phone number for WhatsApp Business
   - Authorize NuCRM to send and receive messages on your behalf
4. Once connected, the status shows "Active" and your phone number is displayed

<Callout variant="note">
The phone number you register for WhatsApp Business cannot be the same number used for a personal WhatsApp account. You will need a dedicated business line.
</Callout>

### Verifying your business

Meta requires business verification before granting API access:

1. Submit your legal business name and registration details
2. Provide a business website URL
3. Upload verification documents if requested
4. Verification typically takes 1-3 business days

## Message templates

WhatsApp requires pre-approved templates for initiating conversations (messages sent when there is no active conversation window).

### Creating a template

1. Go to **Settings > WhatsApp > Templates**
2. Click **Create template**
3. Fill in:
   - **Template name** - Internal identifier
   - **Category** - Marketing, Utility, or Authentication
   - **Language** - Select the message language
   - **Body** - Message content with optional variables (for example, "Hi {{1}}, your appointment is confirmed for {{2}}")
   - **Header** (optional) - Text, image, or document
   - **Footer** (optional) - Brief text at the bottom
   - **Buttons** (optional) - Quick reply or call-to-action buttons
4. Submit for review

### Template approval

WhatsApp reviews all templates before they can be used:

- **Approved** - Ready to send
- **Rejected** - Does not meet WhatsApp policies (you can edit and resubmit)
- **Pending** - Under review (usually 24-48 hours)

<Callout variant="tip">
Follow WhatsApp template guidelines: no promotional content in Utility templates, no misleading information, and always provide clear opt-out options. Templates that violate policies will be rejected.
</Callout>

## Sending WhatsApp messages

### From a contact record

1. Open a contact with a WhatsApp-compatible phone number
2. Click the **WhatsApp** icon in the actions bar (or find it under communication channels)
3. If there is an active conversation window (within 24 hours of their last message), you can type a free-form message
4. If there is no active window, select an approved template
5. Fill in any template variables
6. Click **Send**

### Conversation windows

WhatsApp uses a 24-hour conversation window system:

| Scenario | What you can send |
|----------|------------------|
| Customer messaged within 24 hours | Any message (free-form text, images, documents) |
| No message from customer in 24+ hours | Only approved templates |
| After you send a template and they reply | Free-form messaging for the next 24 hours |

### Message types

Within an active conversation window, send:

- **Text** - Plain text messages
- **Images** - JPG, PNG files with optional caption
- **Documents** - PDF, DOCX, XLSX files
- **Audio** - Voice messages
- **Location** - Send a map pin
- **Quick replies** - Buttons for fast responses

## Receiving messages

### Unified inbox

Incoming WhatsApp messages appear in:

- The **Communication** inbox (unified with email, SMS, and chat)
- The contact's timeline on their detail page
- As a notification in the notification center

### Replying to incoming messages

1. Open the conversation from the Communication inbox or contact timeline
2. Type your reply in the message input
3. Click **Send** (or press Enter)
4. Your reply appears in the conversation thread

<Callout variant="info">
Set up auto-assignment rules to route incoming WhatsApp messages to the correct team member based on the contact's owner, language, or location. This ensures fast response times.
</Callout>

## Bulk WhatsApp messaging

Send template messages to multiple contacts:

1. Go to **Contacts** and filter to your target audience
2. Select the contacts (or use a segment)
3. Click **Send WhatsApp** in bulk actions
4. Select an approved template
5. Map template variables to contact fields (for example, {{1}} = first_name)
6. Preview messages for a few recipients
7. Click **Send campaign**

### Campaign limits

WhatsApp enforces sending limits based on your account tier:

| Tier | Daily limit |
|------|------------|
| Tier 1 (new accounts) | 1,000 messages |
| Tier 2 | 10,000 messages |
| Tier 3 | 100,000 messages |
| Unlimited | No cap (high-quality, established accounts) |

Your tier increases automatically as you maintain high message quality and low spam reports.

<Callout variant="warning">
High spam report rates or low read rates can reduce your tier or get your number blocked. Always send relevant messages to contacts who have opted in to WhatsApp communication.
</Callout>

## WhatsApp analytics

Track messaging performance from **Reports > WhatsApp**:

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Messages sent | Total outbound messages |
| Messages delivered | Successfully delivered to recipient |
| Messages read | Recipient opened and viewed |
| Responses received | Replies from contacts |
| Average response time | How quickly your team replies |
| Template performance | Per-template delivery and read rates |

## Automation with WhatsApp

Integrate WhatsApp into your workflows:

### Trigger-based messages

Set up automated WhatsApp messages for:

- Appointment reminders (24 hours before scheduled meeting)
- Order confirmations
- Welcome messages when a new contact is created
- Follow-up after a missed call

### WhatsApp in sequences

Add WhatsApp steps to multi-channel sequences:

1. Open the sequence editor
2. Add a step with channel = "WhatsApp"
3. Select the template
4. Configure timing (send 2 days after previous step)

## Best practices

1. **Get explicit opt-in** - Only message contacts who have consented to WhatsApp communication
2. **Respond quickly** - WhatsApp users expect fast replies (aim for under 5 minutes during business hours)
3. **Use templates wisely** - Craft templates that provide value, not just promotional content
4. **Maintain quality** - Monitor read rates and spam reports to protect your account tier
5. **Offer an exit** - Always include instructions for opting out of future messages
