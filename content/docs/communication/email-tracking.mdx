## Overview

Email tracking tells you whether recipients opened your messages and clicked any links inside them. This information helps you time follow-ups perfectly, identify engaged prospects, and measure the effectiveness of your outreach. NuCRM provides open tracking, click tracking, and per-email analytics on every message you send.

## How tracking works

### Open tracking

NuCRM embeds a tiny invisible image (tracking pixel) in outgoing emails. When the recipient's email client loads this image, NuCRM records an "open" event with:

- Timestamp of when the email was opened
- Approximate location (city/country based on IP)
- Device type (desktop, mobile, tablet)
- Number of times the email was reopened

### Click tracking

Links in your emails are routed through NuCRM's tracking service. When a recipient clicks a link:

- The click is recorded with a timestamp
- The original URL destination is identified
- The contact is redirected to the intended page seamlessly
- Multiple clicks on the same link are tracked individually

<Callout variant="note">
Some email clients (particularly Apple Mail with privacy settings enabled) may pre-load tracking pixels, which can inflate open counts. NuCRM accounts for this by flagging potential automated opens and providing a "reliable opens" metric that excludes suspected bots.
</Callout>

## Enabling tracking

### Global settings

1. Go to **Settings > Email > Tracking**
2. Configure defaults:
   - **Open tracking** - Enabled by default for all outgoing emails
   - **Click tracking** - Enabled by default for all links
   - **Reply tracking** - Track when recipients reply (always on)
3. Click **Save**

### Per-email override

When composing an email, you can toggle tracking for that specific message:

1. Open the email composer
2. Click the **tracking toggle** at the bottom (eye icon)
3. Enable or disable open/click tracking for this email only
4. Useful for sensitive communications where tracking may be inappropriate

## Viewing tracking data

### On the email record

Each sent email displays tracking indicators:

| Indicator | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| Gray circle | Sent but not yet opened |
| Blue eye icon | Opened (with count if opened multiple times) |
| Green link icon | At least one link was clicked |
| Purple reply icon | Recipient replied to this email |

Click the tracking icons for detailed information:

- Exact open timestamps (multiple opens listed)
- Which links were clicked and when
- Device and location data (when available)

### On the contact timeline

Timeline entries for emails include tracking status:

- "Opened 3 times, last opened 2 hours ago"
- "Clicked 'Pricing page' link at 10:32 AM"
- "Replied at 11:15 AM"

### In the Communication inbox

The email list view shows status indicators:

- Column showing open/click status at a glance
- Filter emails by "opened," "clicked," or "no engagement"
- Sort by most recently opened to identify hot leads

<Callout variant="tip">
Set up a saved view in your Communication inbox filtered to "Opened in last 24 hours but not replied." This gives you a daily list of warm prospects who saw your message but have not responded yet, providing perfect follow-up timing.
</Callout>

## Real-time notifications

Get alerted immediately when a recipient engages:

### Setting up tracking notifications

1. Go to **Settings > Notifications > Email tracking**
2. Enable the notifications you want:
   - **First open** - Notified when the email is first opened
   - **Link click** - Notified when any link is clicked
   - **Re-open** - Notified when opened again after 24+ hours
3. Choose notification channel: in-app, browser push, or both
4. Click **Save**

### When to act on notifications

| Event | Suggested action |
|-------|-----------------|
| Email opened multiple times | Contact is considering your proposal; follow up |
| Pricing link clicked | High intent signal; call immediately |
| Opened after 7+ days of silence | Re-engage with a fresh angle |
| Forwarded (multiple unique opens) | Multiple stakeholders reviewing; prepare for committee |

## Email analytics dashboard

View aggregate tracking data from **Reports > Email analytics**:

### Key metrics

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Open rate | % of sent emails that were opened |
| Click rate | % of opened emails where a link was clicked |
| Reply rate | % of emails that received a response |
| Bounce rate | % that could not be delivered |
| Unsubscribe rate | % who opted out |

### Breakdowns

Analyze performance by:

- **By sender** - Which team member gets the best engagement
- **By template** - Which templates perform highest
- **By day/time** - When emails get the most opens
- **By subject line** - Which subjects drive engagement
- **By recipient segment** - Which audience is most responsive

<Callout variant="info">
The best time to send emails varies by audience. Use the "By day/time" breakdown to identify when your specific contacts are most likely to open. NuCRM can then suggest optimal send times when you schedule emails.
</Callout>

## Link tracking details

### Tracking specific links

For emails with multiple links, see which ones get clicked:

- Each unique URL is tracked separately
- View click counts per link
- Identify the most compelling content or offers
- Use this data to optimize future email layout (put highest-value links near the top)

### Link naming

Name your links for clearer reporting:

1. When inserting a link, add a tracking label
2. Reports show the label instead of the raw URL
3. Examples: "CTA - Book Demo," "Pricing page," "Case study PDF"

## Privacy considerations

### Respecting recipient preferences

- If a contact has requested no tracking, honor that request by disabling tracking on individual emails
- Some organizations block tracking pixels; do not assume non-opens mean non-engagement
- Never use tracking data to pressure or manipulate recipients

### Compliance

- Tracking is standard industry practice for business email
- Include appropriate privacy disclosures if required by your region
- Data is stored securely and only accessible to workspace members with email access

<Callout variant="warning">
Do not make business decisions based solely on open data. Email clients with privacy features, corporate firewalls, and plain-text preferences can all prevent accurate open tracking. Use opens as one signal among many.
</Callout>

## Troubleshooting

### Low open rate

- Check that subject lines are compelling and not triggering spam filters
- Verify your email domain reputation is healthy
- Confirm emails are not landing in spam (ask a test recipient to check)
- Consider that privacy-conscious email clients may block pixels

### Tracking not working

- Verify tracking is enabled in Settings
- Check that the per-email toggle was not accidentally turned off
- Ensure your email domain is properly authenticated (SPF, DKIM, DMARC)

## Best practices

1. **Follow up on opens** - An opened email is permission to follow up (but do not mention you tracked them)
2. **Track link clicks for intent** - Pricing page clicks signal buying intent more than generic opens
3. **Use notifications wisely** - Only enable real-time notifications for high-value prospects to avoid alert fatigue
4. **Measure and improve** - Use analytics to refine your templates and subject lines over time
5. **Respect boundaries** - If someone has not opened five emails in a row, they may not be interested
