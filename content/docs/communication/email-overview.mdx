## Overview

Email is the backbone of sales communication. NuCRM integrates email directly into the platform so you can send messages, track opens and clicks, use templates, and maintain a complete conversation history on every contact record. No need to switch between your inbox and your CRM.

## Email capabilities in NuCRM

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Send email | Compose and send from within NuCRM |
| Two-way sync | Sync with Gmail or Outlook to capture all conversations |
| Templates | Save and reuse common messages |
| Personalization | Insert contact fields dynamically |
| Tracking | Monitor opens, clicks, and replies |
| Scheduling | Send emails at a future time |
| Bulk email | Send to multiple contacts at once |
| Sequences | Automated multi-step follow-up series |

## Sending an email

### From a contact record

1. Open any contact detail page
2. Click **Send email** in the action bar
3. The email composer opens with the recipient pre-filled

### From the command palette

1. Press **Cmd+K** (Mac) or **Ctrl+K** (Windows/Linux)
2. Type "Send email"
3. Select the contact you want to email

### From the Communication inbox

1. Click **Communication** in the sidebar
2. Click **Compose** at the top
3. Start typing the recipient name or email to search your contacts

## The email composer

The composer provides a full-featured editing experience:

### Fields

- **From** - Your connected email address (if you have multiple, select one)
- **To** - Recipient email address (search contacts by name)
- **CC / BCC** - Additional recipients (click CC/BCC to show these fields)
- **Subject** - Email subject line
- **Body** - Rich text message content

### Formatting toolbar

- Bold, italic, underline, strikethrough
- Headings (H1, H2, H3)
- Bullet and numbered lists
- Links (highlight text and add URL)
- Images (upload or paste from clipboard)
- Attachments (click paperclip icon or drag files)
- Code blocks (for technical content)
- Horizontal divider

<Callout variant="tip">
Press Cmd+Enter (Mac) or Ctrl+Enter (Windows) to send the email immediately. This keyboard shortcut works from anywhere in the composer.
</Callout>

### Personalization variables

Insert contact data dynamically:

1. Click the **{ }** icon in the toolbar (or type `{{`)
2. Select a variable from the dropdown:
   - `{{first_name}}` - Contact's first name
   - `{{last_name}}` - Contact's last name
   - `{{company}}` - Contact's company name
   - `{{job_title}}` - Contact's job title
   - `{{deal_name}}` - Related deal name
   - Custom field variables
3. The variable is replaced with the actual value when sent

### Attachments

Attach files to your emails:

- Drag and drop files onto the composer
- Click the paperclip icon to browse your computer
- Maximum attachment size: 25 MB total per email
- Common formats: PDF, DOCX, XLSX, images

## Scheduling emails

Send an email at a future time when it is more likely to be read:

1. Compose your email
2. Instead of clicking **Send**, click the dropdown arrow next to the Send button
3. Select **Schedule send**
4. Choose a date and time (or pick a suggested time like "Tomorrow at 9 AM")
5. Click **Schedule**

The email is held and sent automatically at the scheduled time. You can cancel or edit scheduled emails from **Communication > Scheduled**.

<Callout variant="note">
Scheduling emails in the recipient's local time zone improves open rates. NuCRM can detect the contact's time zone from their location data and suggest optimal send times.
</Callout>

## Email signatures

### Setting up your signature

1. Go to **Settings > Email > Signatures**
2. Click **Create signature**
3. Design your signature using the rich text editor:
   - Name and title
   - Company name
   - Phone number
   - Website link
   - Logo or headshot image
4. Click **Save**

### Multiple signatures

Create different signatures for different contexts:

- Formal (full company details and logo)
- Casual (just name and phone)
- Specific product (includes product-specific links)

Select which signature to use when composing, or set a default.

## Email in the contact timeline

Every email sent or received appears on the contact timeline:

- **Sent emails** show with a paper plane icon and tracking status
- **Received emails** show with an inbox icon
- **Threads** are grouped so replies appear nested under the original message
- Click any email to expand its full content inline

## Email threading

NuCRM groups related emails into conversation threads:

- Original email and all replies appear together
- Thread count badge shows how many messages are in the conversation
- Expand a thread to read the full history
- Reply inline to continue the conversation

## Bulk email

Send personalized emails to multiple contacts:

1. Go to **Contacts** and select the recipients (filter or manually select)
2. Click **Send email** in the bulk actions toolbar
3. Compose your message using personalization variables
4. Preview how it will look for individual recipients
5. Click **Send to all** (or schedule)

<Callout variant="warning">
Bulk email should be used for legitimate business communication with contacts who expect to hear from you. NuCRM is not a mass email marketing tool. For large campaigns (1,000+ recipients), use the dedicated campaign feature with proper unsubscribe handling.
</Callout>

## Email settings

Configure email behavior in **Settings > Email**:

| Setting | Options |
|---------|---------|
| Default from address | Select which connected account sends by default |
| Reply tracking | Enable automatic reply detection |
| Send delay | Add a 5-30 second delay after hitting Send (for catching mistakes) |
| Thread handling | Group by subject line or by header references |
| Auto-BCC | Copy all outbound emails to a specific address |

## Best practices

1. **Connect your email first** - Sync your Gmail or Outlook before sending to maintain history
2. **Use templates for repetition** - If you send similar emails regularly, save them as templates
3. **Personalize every message** - Use variables to avoid generic emails that get ignored
4. **Track responsibly** - Respect recipient privacy and follow email tracking regulations
5. **Review sent emails** - Periodically check your Communication tab to ensure messages were delivered
