## How reminders work

Every task with a due date generates notifications automatically:

- **On the due date** — a notification appears in your inbox at the start of the working day
- **When overdue** — a follow-up notification if the task was not completed by end of day

You do not need to set reminders manually for these; they happen by default.

## Custom reminders

For tasks that need earlier warning, add a custom reminder:

1. Open the task detail
2. Click **Add reminder**
3. Choose when: 1 hour before, 1 day before, 3 days before, or a custom time

Custom reminders are delivered as in-app notifications and, if you have email notifications enabled, by email.

<Callout variant="tip">
Set a 3-day reminder on tasks linked to deals that are in the negotiation stage — it gives you time to prepare rather than scrambling on the day.
</Callout>

## Notification channels

Reminders are delivered through:

- **In-app notifications** — the bell icon in the header, with a live unread count
- **Email digest** — if enabled in your notification preferences
- **Telegram** — if your workspace has the Telegram integration configured

Configure which channels you receive in **Settings > Preferences > Notifications**.

## Overdue task visibility

Managers can see all overdue tasks across their team without configuring anything. The **Overdue** filter on the task list shows every task past its due date, grouped by owner, so nothing hides behind someone's personal queue.
