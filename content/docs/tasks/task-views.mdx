## Three ways to see your tasks

NuCRM offers three views for tasks. Switch between them using the view toggle at the top of the Tasks page.

### List view

The default. A sortable, filterable table showing title, owner, priority, due date, status and linked record. Click any column header to sort. Use the filter bar to narrow by owner, priority, status or date range.

### Kanban view

Columns represent statuses (Open, In Progress, Completed). Drag a task card between columns to change its status. Each card shows the title, owner avatar, priority badge and due date.

<Callout variant="tip">
The kanban view is especially useful in team standups — everyone can see at a glance what is in progress and what is stuck.
</Callout>

### Calendar view

Tasks plotted on a month, week or day calendar by their due date. Drag a task to a different day to reschedule it. Tasks without a due date do not appear here.

## Saved views

Create a saved view by setting your preferred filters and sort order, then clicking **Save view**. Give it a name — it appears in the sidebar under Tasks for quick access. Saved views are personal by default; share them with the team by toggling visibility.

## My tasks vs all tasks

The default filter shows tasks assigned to you. Switch to **All tasks** to see the full team's workload, or filter by a specific team member.
