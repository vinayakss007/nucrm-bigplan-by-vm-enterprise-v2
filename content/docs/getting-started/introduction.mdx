## What is NuCRM?

NuCRM is a customer relationship management platform designed to help growing businesses organize contacts, close more deals, and maintain lasting customer relationships. Whether you run a five-person sales team or manage operations across multiple departments, NuCRM provides the tools you need to track every interaction, automate repetitive work, and gain insights into your revenue pipeline.

Unlike basic spreadsheets or fragmented point solutions, NuCRM brings your contacts, deals, communication history, tasks, and analytics into a single unified workspace. Every team member sees the same up-to-date information, eliminating data silos and miscommunication.

## Who is NuCRM for?

NuCRM serves a wide range of teams and industries:

- **Sales teams** that need to track leads through pipeline stages, forecast revenue, and hit quota
- **Account managers** who maintain long-term relationships and need visibility into renewal dates, support tickets, and communication history
- **Customer success teams** looking to monitor satisfaction scores, proactively reach out, and reduce churn
- **Operations leaders** who want a single source of truth for customer data across departments
- **Small business owners** who handle sales, support, and billing themselves and need one platform that does it all

NuCRM works for teams of any size. You can start with a single user and scale to hundreds of seats as your organization grows.
</Callout>

## Key capabilities

### Contact and company management

Store detailed profiles for every person and organization you work with. Track communication history, notes, custom fields, lifecycle stages, and segment contacts into dynamic lists based on behavior or attributes.

### Visual sales pipeline

Manage your deals on a drag-and-drop kanban board. Create multiple pipelines for different product lines or regions, customize stages and win probabilities, and view forecasted revenue at a glance.

### Multi-channel communication

Send and receive emails directly from NuCRM with full two-way sync for Gmail and Outlook. Use built-in WhatsApp and SMS messaging. Every conversation is automatically logged on the relevant contact timeline.

### Automation engine

Build visual workflows that trigger actions when contacts move stages, deals close, emails are opened, or custom conditions are met. Automate lead assignment, follow-up sequences, and task creation to save hours of manual work every week.

### Quotes, invoices, and billing

Generate professional quotes and invoices, track payments, and manage subscriptions without leaving the CRM. Multi-currency support and tax configuration are built in.

### Support and helpdesk

Create and manage support tickets, define SLAs, and give customers a self-service portal. Route tickets to the right agents automatically and measure resolution times.

### Reports and dashboards

Build custom reports with a visual report builder. Pin charts to dashboards that update in real time, schedule email deliveries to stakeholders, and drill down into any metric.

### AI-powered insights

Let the AI assistant draft emails, score leads, predict deal outcomes, and summarize lengthy conversations so your team spends less time on admin and more time selling.
