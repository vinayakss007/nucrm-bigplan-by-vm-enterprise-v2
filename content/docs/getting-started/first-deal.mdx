## Overview

Deals represent sales opportunities in NuCRM. Each deal tracks a potential revenue amount, the contacts involved, the current stage in your pipeline, and every activity leading toward a closed sale. This guide shows you how to create your first deal and move it through the pipeline.

## Creating a new deal

### From the pipeline view

1. Click **Deals** in the sidebar to open the pipeline kanban board
2. Click the **+ New deal** button in the top-right corner
3. The deal creation form opens as a slide-over panel

### From a contact record

1. Open any contact detail page
2. Click the **Create deal** button in the actions bar
3. The contact is automatically linked to the new deal

### From the command palette

1. Press **Cmd+K** (Mac) or **Ctrl+K** (Windows/Linux)
2. Type "New deal" or "Create deal"
3. Press **Enter**

## The deal creation form

Fill in the following fields when creating a deal:

### Required fields

| Field | Description | Example |
|-------|------------|---------|
| Deal name | A descriptive name for this opportunity | Acme Corp - Enterprise Plan |
| Pipeline | Which pipeline this deal belongs to | Default Sales Pipeline |
| Stage | Current stage in the pipeline | Qualified |
| Deal value | Expected revenue amount | $24,000 |

### Recommended fields

- **Contact** - The primary person you are negotiating with
- **Company** - The organization purchasing
- **Close date** - Expected date the deal will close (won or lost)
- **Deal owner** - The team member responsible for this deal
- **Currency** - Defaults to your workspace currency, can be overridden

### Creating the deal

1. Fill in the required fields
2. Add any relevant optional fields
3. Click **Create deal**
4. The deal card appears in the selected stage on your pipeline board

<Callout variant="tip">
Be specific with deal names. Instead of "New opportunity," use a format like "Company - Product - Value" so deals are instantly recognizable on the kanban board.
</Callout>

## Understanding the pipeline

Your pipeline is a visual representation of your sales process. Deals flow from left to right through stages:

### Default stages

NuCRM provides a default pipeline with these stages (you can customize later):

1. **Lead** - Initial interest identified
2. **Qualified** - Budget, authority, need, and timeline confirmed
3. **Proposal** - Quote or proposal sent to the prospect
4. **Negotiation** - Terms are being discussed
5. **Closed Won** - Deal successfully closed
6. **Closed Lost** - Deal did not result in a sale

### Moving deals between stages

You can advance a deal in two ways:

**Drag and drop** (Kanban view):
1. Click and hold a deal card
2. Drag it to the target stage column
3. Release to drop it in place

**From the deal detail page**:
1. Open the deal by clicking its name
2. Click the **Stage** dropdown at the top
3. Select the new stage
4. The change is saved immediately

<Callout variant="note">
When you move a deal to a new stage, NuCRM logs the transition on the deal timeline with a timestamp. This data powers your pipeline velocity reports and helps identify deals that are stuck too long in a single stage.
</Callout>

## The deal detail view

Click any deal card on the pipeline board to open its detail view:

### Key sections

- **Header** - Deal name, value, stage, close date, and owner
- **Timeline** - All activities, emails, notes, and stage changes in chronological order
- **Contacts** - People associated with this deal (you can link multiple contacts)
- **Tasks** - Follow-up tasks and to-dos related to the deal
- **Documents** - Attached proposals, contracts, and files
- **Activity log** - Calls, meetings, and notes logged against this deal

### Deal health indicators

NuCRM shows visual indicators for deal health:

- **Green** - Active, engaged, progressing normally
- **Yellow** - No activity in the last 7 days
- **Red** - No activity in 14+ days or past the expected close date

## Logging activities on a deal

Keep your deal timeline active by logging interactions:

### Log a call

1. Open the deal detail page
2. Click **Log call** in the actions bar
3. Enter the call summary, duration, and outcome
4. Click **Save**

### Add a note

1. Click **Add note** on the deal page
2. Write your meeting summary, key takeaways, or next steps
3. Click **Save**

### Schedule a follow-up

1. Click **Create task** from the deal page
2. Enter the task title (for example, "Send revised proposal")
3. Set a due date
4. Assign it to yourself or a teammate
5. Click **Create**

<Callout variant="info">
Deals with consistent activity logs have higher close rates. Aim to log at least one meaningful interaction per deal per week to keep momentum and identify when engagement drops.
</Callout>

## Closing a deal

When a deal reaches its conclusion:

### Won deal

1. Move the deal to the **Closed Won** stage
2. NuCRM prompts you to confirm the final deal value
3. Optionally add a won reason from the dropdown (for example, "Best price," "Product fit," "Existing relationship")
4. The deal is marked as won and contributes to your revenue reports

### Lost deal

1. Move the deal to the **Closed Lost** stage
2. Select a lost reason (for example, "Went with competitor," "Budget constraints," "No decision made")
3. Optionally add a note explaining the loss
4. The deal is archived but remains searchable and included in win/loss analytics
