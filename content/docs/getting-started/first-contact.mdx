## Overview

Contacts are the foundation of NuCRM. Every deal, email, task, and activity links back to a contact record. This guide walks you through creating your first contact, filling in key details, and understanding the timeline that automatically tracks every interaction.

## Creating a new contact

There are multiple ways to create a contact:

### From the sidebar

1. Click **Contacts** in the sidebar
2. Click the **+ New contact** button in the top-right corner
3. The contact creation form opens

### From the command palette

1. Press **Cmd+K** (Mac) or **Ctrl+K** (Windows/Linux)
2. Type "Create contact"
3. Press **Enter**

### From anywhere (quick action)

1. Click the **+** button in the top bar
2. Select **Contact**

## Filling in contact details

The contact form has required fields (marked with an asterisk) and optional fields you can complete now or later:

### Required fields

| Field | Description | Example |
|-------|------------|---------|
| First name | Contact's given name | Sarah |
| Last name | Contact's family name | Johnson |
| Email | Primary email address | sarah.johnson@acme.com |

### Recommended fields

- **Phone** - Primary phone number with country code
- **Company** - Link to an existing company or create one inline
- **Job title** - Their role at the organization
- **Lifecycle stage** - Where they are in your funnel (Lead, Prospect, Customer)

### Adding the contact

1. Fill in the required and any additional fields
2. Click **Save contact**
3. NuCRM creates the record and opens the contact detail view

<Callout variant="tip">
If you add a company name that does not yet exist in NuCRM, the system will offer to create a new company record and automatically link the contact to it.
</Callout>

## The contact detail view

After creating a contact, you land on their detail page, which has several sections:

### Profile header

The top section shows:

- Contact name and avatar (auto-generated from initials or pulled from Gravatar)
- Job title and company name (clickable link to the company record)
- Primary email and phone (click to copy or initiate communication)
- Lifecycle stage badge
- Lead score indicator

### Information panel

Below the header, organized in tabs:

- **Details** - All fields including custom fields, organized in a two-column layout
- **Activity** - Timeline of all interactions (see below)
- **Deals** - Any deals associated with this contact
- **Tasks** - Open and completed tasks linked to this contact
- **Emails** - Full email conversation history
- **Notes** - Internal notes from your team
- **Files** - Attached documents and files

## Adding a note

Notes are internal comments visible only to your team. They are useful for capturing context from phone calls, meeting summaries, or observations:

1. From the contact detail view, click the **Notes** tab (or use the quick note icon on the activity tab)
2. Click **Add note**
3. Type your note in the rich text editor (supports bold, italic, bullet lists, and @mentions of team members)
4. Click **Save**

The note appears immediately on the contact timeline with your name and timestamp.

<Callout variant="note">
Use @mentions in notes to notify a colleague. For example, typing "@David" will send David a notification that he was mentioned on this contact record.
</Callout>

## Understanding the timeline

The timeline is a chronological record of every interaction with a contact, displayed on the Activity tab:

### What appears on the timeline

- Emails sent and received
- Notes added by team members
- Phone calls logged
- Meetings scheduled and completed
- Deal creation and stage changes
- Task completion
- Form submissions
- Lifecycle stage transitions
- WhatsApp and SMS messages

### Filtering the timeline

Use the filter buttons above the timeline to show only specific activity types:

- **All** - Everything in chronological order
- **Emails** - Only email interactions
- **Notes** - Only internal notes
- **Calls** - Only logged phone calls
- **Meetings** - Only meeting records

### Timeline actions

From any timeline entry, you can:

- **Reply** to an email directly inline
- **Edit** a note you wrote
- **Pin** important entries to the top of the timeline
- **Delete** entries you created (requires appropriate permissions)

## Editing contact information

To update any field on a contact:

1. Click the **Details** tab on the contact page
2. Click on any field value to make it editable
3. Make your change
4. Press **Enter** or click away to save (changes save automatically)

<Callout variant="info">
All edits are tracked in the timeline. If a field is changed, NuCRM logs who changed it, when, and what the previous value was. This audit trail is helpful for understanding how contact information evolved over time.
</Callout>

## Quick actions on contacts

From the contact detail page, use the action buttons in the header:

- **Send email** - Opens the email composer pre-filled with the contact's address
- **Log call** - Record details about a phone conversation
- **Create task** - Add a follow-up task linked to this contact
- **Create deal** - Start a new deal associated with this contact
- **Schedule meeting** - Set up a meeting with calendar integration
