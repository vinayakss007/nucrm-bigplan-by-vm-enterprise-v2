## Overview

Companies (also called organizations or accounts) represent the businesses you work with. In NuCRM, a company record aggregates all contacts at that organization, their collective deal history, and total revenue. This guide covers creating companies, linking contacts, and using company hierarchies.

## Creating a company

### From the sidebar

1. Click **Companies** in the sidebar
2. Click **+ New company** in the top-right corner
3. Fill in the company creation form

### Inline from a contact

When editing a contact's company field:

1. Type the company name
2. If it does not exist, click **Create "Company Name"**
3. The company is created and linked automatically

### Company fields

| Field | Description | Example |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| Company name | Official organization name | Acme Corporation |
| Domain | Primary website domain | acme.com |
| Industry | Business sector | Software & Technology |
| Size | Employee count range | 51-200 |
| Annual revenue | Estimated yearly revenue | $5,000,000 |
| Phone | Main company phone | +1 (555) 100-2000 |
| Address | Headquarters address | 123 Main St, San Francisco, CA |
| Owner | Team member managing this account | David Miller |

<Callout variant="tip">
Enter the company domain (website URL) when creating a company. NuCRM can auto-enrich basic company information like logo, industry, and size from publicly available data.
</Callout>

## The company detail page

The company detail view provides an aggregated overview:

### Header section

- Company name and logo (fetched from domain or uploaded manually)
- Industry and size badges
- Total revenue (sum of all closed-won deals)
- Total contacts count
- Active deals count

### Contacts list

All people linked to this company, showing:

- Name and job title
- Email and phone
- Their lifecycle stage
- Last activity date

Add new contacts directly from the company page by clicking **Add contact** and either linking an existing record or creating a new one.

### Deals section

All deals associated with this company through linked contacts:

- Open deals with stage and projected value
- Won deals with close date
- Lost deals with reason
- Total deal pipeline value

### Activity timeline

Aggregated timeline showing all interactions across all contacts at this company:

- Emails exchanged with anyone at the company
- Meetings held with company contacts
- Notes and calls logged
- Deal progression events

<Callout variant="note">
The company timeline combines activity from all linked contacts, giving you a complete picture of your relationship with the organization without needing to check each contact individually.
</Callout>

## Linking contacts to companies

### Automatic linking

NuCRM can automatically link contacts to companies based on email domain:

1. Go to **Settings > Contacts > Auto-linking**
2. Enable **Link contacts by email domain**
3. When a new contact is created with an email like `john@acme.com`, NuCRM checks for a company with the domain `acme.com` and links them automatically

### Manual linking

1. Open a contact record
2. Click the **Company** field
3. Search for and select the company
4. Click **Save**

### Unlinking

1. Open the contact record
2. Click the X next to the company name in the Company field
3. The link is removed but neither record is deleted

## Company hierarchy

For organizations with parent-subsidiary relationships:

### Setting up hierarchy

1. Open the child company record
2. Find the **Parent company** field
3. Search for and select the parent company
4. Click **Save**

### Hierarchy view

From a parent company page:

- A **Subsidiaries** section shows all child companies
- Revenue rolls up to show the total across all entities
- Contact count includes all subsidiary contacts

### Use cases for hierarchy

- Corporate groups with regional offices
- Franchise organizations with individual locations
- Holding companies with portfolio businesses

<Callout variant="info">
You can create up to three levels of hierarchy: parent, child, and grandchild. This covers most organizational structures without overcomplicating navigation.
</Callout>

## Company list view

The Companies list works similarly to the Contacts list:

### Filtering

Filter companies by:
- Industry
- Size (employee range)
- Annual revenue range
- Number of contacts
- Number of active deals
- Owner
- Tags
- Custom fields

### Sorting

Sort by company name, creation date, total revenue, number of deals, or last activity date.

### Saved views

Create and save filtered views like:
- "Enterprise accounts (200+ employees)"
- "Accounts with no active deals"
- "My accounts in Healthcare"

## Bulk operations on companies

Select multiple companies to:

- Assign or change owner
- Add or remove tags
- Update industry or other fields
- Export to CSV
- Delete (moves to trash)

## Company enrichment

NuCRM can fill in missing company data from publicly available information:

1. Ensure the company has a valid domain entered
2. Click the **Enrich** button on the company detail page (or enable auto-enrichment in Settings)
3. Available data is populated: logo, description, industry, approximate size, social links, and founded year

<Callout variant="tip">
Enable auto-enrichment for new companies in Settings > Companies to automatically pull in public data whenever a company record is created. This saves manual data entry time.
</Callout>

## Merging duplicate companies

If you discover two records for the same organization:

1. Open one of the company records
2. Click the three-dot menu and select **Merge with another company**
3. Search for the duplicate record
4. Review the merge preview showing which fields will be kept from each record
5. Click **Merge**

All contacts, deals, and activities from both records are combined into the surviving record.
