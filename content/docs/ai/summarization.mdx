## When to summarise

You are about to jump on a call with a contact you have not spoken to in weeks. Their record has 40 emails, 6 call notes and 3 tickets. Reading everything takes 20 minutes. A summary takes 5 seconds and gives you:

- Key recent events and outcomes
- Current status of any open deals
- Sentiment across recent communication
- Outstanding tasks or follow-ups

## Using summarisation

Click the **Summarise** button on any:

- **Contact detail** — summarises the full relationship history
- **Deal detail** — summarises negotiation progress, blockers and next steps
- **Ticket thread** — summarises the issue, what has been tried, and current status

The summary appears in a panel and can be pinned to the record as a note.

<Callout variant="tip">
Summarise before a meeting. Pin the summary as a note dated today. After the meeting, your notes build on what the AI provided instead of starting cold.
</Callout>

## What it reads

The summariser accesses:

- All activities and notes on the record
- Email subjects and recent message content
- Call logs and their notes
- Linked ticket statuses and resolutions
- Field changes (stage movements, value changes)

It does not access records you do not have permission to view.

## Credit cost

Summaries cost 1–3 credits depending on the volume of history being processed. A contact with 5 activities costs less than one with 200.
