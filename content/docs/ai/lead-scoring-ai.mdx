## Two scoring layers

NuCRM scores leads using two complementary approaches:

1. **Rule-based scoring** — you define the criteria (BANT, field values, activity thresholds) and the points each contributes
2. **AI-assisted scoring** — the model analyses engagement patterns, record completeness, timing signals and behavioural data to add or subtract from the rule-based score

The final score (0–100) is a blend of both, and the breakdown is visible on every lead so reps can see *why* a lead is hot, not just that it is.

## Setting up rule-based scoring

Go to **Settings > Lead scoring** and define your rules:

- **Fit signals** — industry match (+10), company size (+5), job title contains "VP" (+8)
- **Activity signals** — opened 3+ emails (+15), visited pricing page (+12), submitted a form (+20)
- **Decay** — no activity in 14 days (−10), 30 days (−20)

Each rule has a weight. The sum produces the rule-based component.

## AI scoring layer

The AI layer runs on a schedule (configurable: daily, twice daily, or triggered by activity). It analyses:

- Engagement velocity (are interactions increasing or declining?)
- Record completeness (leads with more data tend to convert at higher rates)
- Pattern similarity to past won deals
- Timing signals (recently active leads score higher)

<Callout variant="note">
The AI score is always explainable. Click the score on any lead to see the top contributing signals — e.g. "High engagement velocity", "Similar profile to 4 recent conversions", "Budget field filled".
</Callout>

## Acting on scores

- **Hot leads (80–100)** — surface in the dashboard "Focus" widget, trigger a notification to the owner
- **Warm leads (50–79)** — work normally, monitor for movement
- **Cold leads (0–49)** — candidate for nurture sequences or reassignment

Use automation to route high-score leads to senior reps, or to enrol low-score leads in warming sequences automatically.
