## Credit system

Every AI action consumes credits from your workspace's monthly budget:

| Action | Typical cost |
|---|---|
| Email draft | 1–2 credits |
| Lead scoring (batch) | 1 credit per 10 leads |
| Summarisation | 1–3 credits |
| Sentiment analysis | 0.5 credits per message |
| Content generation | 2–4 credits |

Your remaining budget is visible in:

- The AI workspace header
- The email composer (before you draft)
- **Settings > AI > Usage** (full breakdown by user and feature)

When credits are exhausted, AI features show a notification instead of producing output. They resume at the start of the next billing period, or when you top up.

## Bring your own key

If you prefer to use your own AI provider account:

1. Go to **Settings > AI providers**
2. Add your provider key (supports multiple providers)
3. Choose which features should use your key vs the platform allowance

When your own key is configured, those requests go directly to your provider account under your terms. The workspace credit budget is not consumed for those calls.

<Callout variant="info">
Bring-your-own-key means the platform never sees your prompts or responses for those calls. Useful for organisations with strict data residency requirements.
</Callout>

## Admin controls

Administrators can:

- **Set a monthly budget** per workspace
- **View usage** broken down by user, feature and date
- **Manage prompt templates** that guide AI output (keeping it on-brand)
- **Disable AI features** entirely at workspace level
- **Restrict which roles** can use AI features

## Audit trail

Every AI action is logged:

- Who triggered it
- Which feature was used
- How many credits it consumed
- The record it was applied to

This log is accessible from **Settings > AI > Usage log** and is included in the workspace audit trail.
