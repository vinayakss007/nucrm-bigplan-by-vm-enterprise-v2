## What the assistant does

The AI assistant handles the administrative work that sits between knowing what to do and having done it: drafting the follow-up you keep postponing, scoring the lead you have not looked at yet, summarising the thread you need to catch up on before a call, and flagging the account that stopped replying three weeks ago.

It does not send anything on your behalf unless you explicitly enable an automation that does so. Every output is presented for your review first.

## Where it appears

| Location | What it does |
|---|---|
| Email composer | Drafts a reply or follow-up from the record's context |
| Contact detail | Summarises the contact, scores them, detects sentiment |
| Deal detail | Predicts win probability, suggests next steps |
| Ticket detail | Summarises the thread, detects urgency |
| Lead list | Scores leads 0–100 and explains the reasoning |
| AI workspace | A dedicated space for drafting, summarising and analysis |
| Automation actions | Can trigger AI drafting, scoring or warming on a schedule |

## What it does not do

- It does not access data outside your workspace
- It does not send messages without your explicit approval (unless you build an automation that enables it)
- It does not make irreversible changes to records
- It does not learn from one workspace and apply that to another

<Callout variant="info">
Every AI action is written to the audit trail with the user, the feature used, and the credit cost. Your administrators can see exactly what was generated and by whom.
</Callout>

## Governance

- **Credits** — each workspace has a monthly AI credit budget, visible in Settings
- **Provider keys** — you can supply your own provider key so calls go to your account
- **Prompt templates** — admins can manage the system prompts that guide output, keeping it on-brand
- **Kill switch** — AI features can be disabled entirely at workspace level
