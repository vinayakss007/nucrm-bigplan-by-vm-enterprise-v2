## What sentiment analysis does

Every inbound message (email, chat, WhatsApp) is optionally scored for sentiment: positive, neutral or negative. The score attaches to the message on the timeline and rolls up to the contact level as an overall sentiment trend.

## Enabling sentiment

Sentiment analysis is part of the AI Assistant module. To enable:

1. Go to **Settings > AI**
2. Toggle **Sentiment analysis** on
3. Choose which channels to analyse (email, chat, WhatsApp, all)

Once enabled, new inbound messages are scored automatically. It does not retroactively score historical messages unless you trigger a manual rescore.

## Reading sentiment

On the contact timeline, each inbound message shows a small sentiment indicator (green/grey/red dot). The contact's overall sentiment is summarised at the top of their detail view:

- **Positive trend** — recent messages are consistently positive
- **Neutral** — mixed or informational tone
- **Negative trend** — recent messages carry frustration, urgency or dissatisfaction

<Callout variant="warning">
Sentiment analysis is a signal, not a diagnosis. A single negative message after a billing error does not mean the customer is churning. Look at the trend over the last 5–10 messages.
</Callout>

## Acting on sentiment

Combine sentiment with automation:

- Trigger a notification to the account owner when a contact's sentiment turns negative
- Create an at-risk flag when 3+ consecutive messages score negative
- Surface negative-sentiment contacts in a saved view for proactive outreach

## Accuracy and limits

Sentiment works best on:

- Messages longer than one sentence
- English text (other languages are supported but with lower accuracy)
- Direct communication (not forwarded threads or automated replies)

Short responses like "OK" or "Thanks" typically score neutral regardless of context.
