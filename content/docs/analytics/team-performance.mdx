## What is measured

Team performance reporting tracks three layers:

1. **Activity** — calls made, emails sent, meetings held, tasks completed
2. **Conversion** — leads qualified, deals advanced, deals won, deal cycle time
3. **Revenue** — closed revenue, average deal size, pipeline contribution

Each metric is available per rep, per team and per period.

## Viewing performance

Go to **Reports > Team performance** or check the leaderboard widget on the sales dashboard.

### Leaderboard

A ranked view of reps by a single metric you choose (closed revenue, deals won, activities logged). Updated in real time. Useful on a wall display for motivation, or in a 1:1 for context.

### Comparison table

A table with every rep as a row and key metrics as columns. Sort by any column to spot outliers — both top performers and those who may need support.

### Trend charts

Line charts showing each rep's metrics over time. Useful for spotting:

- A rep whose activity dropped suddenly (might be stuck or burned out)
- A rep whose conversion rate is climbing (doing something worth sharing)
- Seasonal patterns in the team's output

<Callout variant="tip">
Use performance data for coaching conversations, not surveillance. "Your conversion rate dipped last month — what changed?" is more productive than a ranked list on a wall with no context.
</Callout>

## Configuring which metrics to show

Go to **Settings > Reports > Team performance** and choose which metrics appear by default. You can add custom metrics based on calculated fields or specific pipeline stages.

## Export

Export the performance table as CSV or PDF for offline reviews, board reporting or compensation discussions.
