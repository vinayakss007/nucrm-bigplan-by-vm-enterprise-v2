## Why schedule reports

Some reports matter every Monday: pipeline coverage, overdue tasks, SLA compliance. Instead of remembering to run them, schedule delivery and they arrive before the stand-up starts.

## Setting up a schedule

1. Open any saved report
2. Click **Schedule**
3. Choose frequency: daily, weekly (pick the day), or monthly (pick the date)
4. Choose time of day
5. Choose format: PDF or CSV
6. Add recipients: yourself, specific team members, or an email address outside the workspace
7. Save

<Callout variant="tip">
Schedule the pipeline report for Monday 07:00 and the revenue summary for the 1st of each month. Two schedules, two clicks, and weekly reporting stops being a manual ritual.
</Callout>

## Execution history

Every scheduled delivery is logged under **Reports > Scheduled > History**:

- When it ran
- Who it was sent to
- Whether delivery succeeded or failed
- A link to view the exact output that was sent

## Pausing and editing

Pause a schedule without deleting it by toggling it off. Edit the frequency, recipients or format at any time — changes apply from the next scheduled run.

## Who receives what

Recipients receive only the data the report shows. If the report filters to a specific team, the recipient sees only that team's data even if they have broader permissions in the workspace. The schedule respects the report's filter, not the recipient's role.

<Callout variant="warning">
Be thoughtful about who receives which scheduled reports. A revenue summary sent to the whole company every week is a different thing to one sent only to the leadership team. Visibility controls exist for a reason.
</Callout>
