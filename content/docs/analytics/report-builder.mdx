## Building a report

Go to **Reports** in the sidebar and click **New report**.

### Step 1: Choose an entity

Select what you are reporting on: contacts, leads, deals, tasks, tickets, invoices, activities or a custom entity.

### Step 2: Set filters

Narrow the data:

- Date range (created, updated, closed)
- Owner or team
- Stage, status, priority
- Tags, custom field values
- Any combination using AND/OR logic

### Step 3: Choose grouping

Group results by any field: stage, owner, source, month, tag, company, custom picklist. Grouping determines how totals and subtotals are calculated.

### Step 4: Select columns

Pick which fields appear as columns. Drag to reorder. For numeric fields, choose whether to show sum, average, count or max.

### Step 5: Preview and save

Click **Preview** to see the data. Adjust filters or columns as needed. When it looks right, click **Save** — give it a name, choose whether it is personal or shared with the team, and optionally assign it to a folder.

<Callout variant="tip">
Start with a question. "Which sources produce deals over $50k?" is a filter (source, value > 50000), a grouping (by source), and a column (count + sum). Working backwards from the question makes the builder straightforward.
</Callout>

## Editing and duplicating

Open any saved report and click **Edit** to change its filters, grouping or columns. Click **Duplicate** to create a variant without losing the original.

## Exporting

From any report, click **Export** and choose:

- **CSV** — raw data, opens in any spreadsheet
- **PDF** — formatted, suitable for sharing with people who do not have a login

## Sharing

Reports can be:

- **Personal** — visible only to you
- **Team-shared** — visible to anyone in the workspace
- **Role-restricted** — visible only to specific roles (e.g. managers and above)
