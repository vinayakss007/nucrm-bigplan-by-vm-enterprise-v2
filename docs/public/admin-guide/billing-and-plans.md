# Billing & Plans

Manage your workspace's subscription, understand plan limits, and handle payments.

> 👤 **Tenant admin** — requires an admin/owner role. Manage in **Settings → Billing**.

---

## Plans

Your workspace is on a **plan** (for example free, starter, pro, or enterprise). The plan
determines:

- **Feature access** — which capabilities are unlocked.
- **Resource limits** — caps on things like users, contacts, deals, automations, storage, API
  calls, and AI credits.

When you approach or exceed a limit, NuCRM shows a plan-limit banner and may block the action until
you upgrade or free up capacity. Enterprise plans are typically unlimited.

### Upgrading or downgrading

Change plans from **Settings → Billing**. Upgrades unlock features and higher limits immediately;
downgrades apply according to your billing cycle.

---

## Trials

New workspaces may start on a **trial**. As the trial nears its end you'll be reminded, and when it
expires the workspace enters a restricted state until you choose a plan. See the trial banner and
**Settings → Billing** to convert.

---

## Invoices & payments

Your subscription invoices and payment history are available in **Settings → Billing**. Payments
are recorded against each invoice, and the balance and status update automatically.

### Payment providers

NuCRM supports multiple payment gateways so it works globally:

- **Stripe**
- **Razorpay**
- **PayU**

Which provider processes your payments depends on how the platform operator has configured your
region. You'll be directed to the appropriate secure checkout.

---

## Usage & limits

Track your consumption against plan limits in the billing/usage views:

- Users, contacts, deals, automations, storage, API calls, AI credits.
- Alerts warn you before you hit a limit.
