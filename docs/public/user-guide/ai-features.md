# AI Features

NuCRM includes AI assistance across the product to help you write faster, understand customers, and
prioritize work.

---

## What AI can do

| Feature | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Email drafting** | Generate email drafts from the context of a contact, deal, or thread. |
| **Content generation** | Produce articles, templates, and marketing copy. |
| **Summarization** | Auto-summarize contacts, deals, and tickets so you catch up fast. |
| **Lead scoring** | ML-assisted scoring layered on top of your custom rules. |
| **Sentiment analysis** | Gauge the sentiment of customer communications. |
| **At-risk / churn detection** | Predict which customers are at risk so you can intervene. |
| **Activity insights** | Recommendations on what to do next. |

---

## AI credits

AI usage is metered per workspace using **AI credits**:

- Each workspace has a credit balance and budget.
- Usage is tracked so admins can see consumption and set limits.
- Check your balance and activity in the **AI** area of the app.

If your workspace runs low on credits, AI features are limited until credits are replenished or
limits are adjusted by an admin.

---

## AI providers

AI is powered by large-language-model providers (for example OpenAI or Claude). Your admin
configures which provider(s) to use and supplies the provider credentials in the workspace's AI
settings. Provider secrets are stored securely and never exposed to end users.

---

## Using AI responsibly

- AI **drafts and suggestions** are starting points — always review before sending.
- AI processes the record context you give it; follow your organization's data-handling policies.
